Solar Power Analysis Dashboard

Data Overview

This project uses two CSV datasets containing historical operational data from a solar power plant. Together, these datasets capture the daily behavior, efficiency, and performance patterns of the plant. The data includes electrical measurements, environmental factors, and calculated yield metrics essential for understanding and forecasting solar output.

Key Features in the Dataset

  • DC Power (kW)
    Raw power generated directly by the solar panels.
  • AC Power (kW)
    Usable electrical output after inversion, representing actual deliverable energy.
  • Module Yield / Average Yield
    Indicates how efficiently the modules convert sunlight into energy.
  • Total Yield
    Cumulative energy generated over time, used to analyze long-term trends.
  • Environmental or Time-Based Columns
